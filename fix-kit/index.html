Questions about the Fix Kit.

Is HomeTally a law firm or a tax consultant?

No. HomeTally is not a law firm or a licensed property tax consultant; the Fix Kit is a do-it-yourself tool. It prepares forms and letters from your numbers; you review, sign and file them yourself. For legal advice, talk to a Texas attorney.

What do I get with the $39 Fix Kit?

In-browser tools that turn your checker results into paperwork: a filled Form 50-869 motion (when your home is valued more than 25% over what you paid), a protest evidence packet, a homestead exemption guide, an escrow dispute letter to your servicer, and a deadline calendar. Everything downloads as PDF or .ics on your device.

Do I need the checker first?

It helps. The Fix Kit fills itself in from your checker results in this tab. You can also type the numbers yourself.

Is my card safe?

HomeTally never asks for or stores card numbers. Payments go through Stripe's own secure checkout page. Until online payment is switched on, the buy buttons only save your name and email so we can send you a payment link.

Do my letters get uploaded?

No. The checker and the Fix Kit read your letters and numbers on your device. The only things sent are the boxes you fill in on a form (like your name and email), and, if you use it, the address you type for the county value lookup.

What if the Fix Kit doesn't fit my situation?

Email laurent@kuumba.app and tell us what happened. While we test, every message gets a personal reply.
