How to get this

A. Your appraisal page (Tarrant Appraisal District)

  1. Open www.tad.org and search your address or account number.
  2. Open your property page and wait until the Values table shows.
  3. Select everything on the page (Ctrl+A, or ⌘+A on a Mac; on a phone, long-press and tap "Select all") and copy it.
  4. Paste it in box A below.

B. Your tax account (Tarrant County Tax Office)

  1. Open tax.tarrantcountytx.gov (main site: tarrantcountytx.gov property tax) and search your account number or address.
  2. Open Account Details, scroll so the payment history has loaded, select all and copy.
  3. Paste it in box B below.

No luck copying? Skip the boxes and type the numbers under "Type the numbers instead".
